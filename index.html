AI is already inside your organization.

The question is how much of it you can see. This briefing walks through what's actually being adopted, where visibility breaks down, and the order in which security closes the gap.

01 — LANDSCAPE

AI doesn't arrive as one thing

It arrives in layers — roughly in order of how invisibly to disruptively each one enters an organization. By the time most security teams notice the third layer, the first two have usually been running for months.

85%of organizations already have AI agents running in production.

02 — EXPOSURE

You can't govern what you can't see

Sanctioned means approved, contracted, governed. Unsanctioned — shadow AI — means personal accounts, browser extensions, ungoverned agent plugins. But the sharper risk isn't the unapproved tool. It's a sanctioned tool used in an unsanctioned way — an approved seat, but someone pastes a client contract into it anyway. Sanctioned vs. unsanctioned is a data-flow question, not a license question.
