02 — EXPOSURE

Harvest Now, Decrypt Later

The commonly repeated framing — "quantum computers are coming, get ready" — undersells the timeline. Intercepted, encrypted traffic can be stored today and decrypted retroactively the moment a capable enough quantum computer exists. For anything that needs to stay confidential for years — health records, state secrets, IP, financial data — the exposure window isn't in the future. It already opened.
