WHAT WOULD HAVE STOPPED THIS

The same four phases, in reverse

Every stage of this scenario maps to one of the guardrail phases from the main briefing. None of them require stopping AI adoption — each one just closes the gap a little earlier.

01 · Discover
Would have caught Day 0

An inventory sweep flags svc-atlas-agent the day it's provisioned — before it ever touches a second system.

02 · Contain
Would have caught Day 3

Least-privilege boundaries block the scope creep — cross-region order data was never in the original approval.

03 · Govern Identity
Would have caught Day 12

Behavioral monitoring on the non-human identity flags 214 calls in 90 seconds — not 45 days later.

04 · Institutionalize
Would have caught it sooner, every time

A standing review cadence for AI identities turns this into a same-week catch instead of a finance-driven surprise.
